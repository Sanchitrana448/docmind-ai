## Reading these numbers honestly

- Documents are synthetic and templated, so accuracy on real, messy documents will be lower. The useful number is the silent-error count, not the accuracy.
- Only the total and the date are scored. Vendor, merchant and CV fields are heuristic and are expected to be wrong more often; they carry low confidence so they get reviewed.
- Review volume: 20 of 32 documents were flagged, of which 10 had a correct total and date (flagged because a weaker field, such as the merchant name, scored below the threshold).
